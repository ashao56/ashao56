### Featured Projects

#### [SPACE Program Evaluation](https://github.com/ashao56/space-program-evaluation)
Led the quantitative half of a mixed-methods evaluation of a $9.3M Boston small-business grant program for the City's Office of Economic Opportunity and Inclusion, delivered to the Mayor's Office. Used an intent-to-treat regression across three specifications to isolate the program's causal effect on storefront openings (23-26 pt increase), plus equity analysis of outcomes by race and business type. Includes a generalized version of the fuzzy-matching pipeline used to clean multi-round administrative data.
`R` `Causal Inference` `Program Evaluation`

#### [Predictive Modeling: CART & Ensemble Methods](https://github.com/ashao56/predictive-modeling-cart-ensembles)
Compared classification trees, random forests, and gradient boosting across a health-risk prediction task and a housing-price prediction task, with cross-validated tuning throughout. Central finding: model choice should depend on whether the audience needs interpretability (e.g. explaining a result to a non-technical stakeholder) or raw predictive accuracy — a trade-off that comes up constantly in applied policy work.
`R` `Machine Learning` `Cross-Validation`

#### [SNAP Benefits Navigator](https://github.com/yourusername/snap-benefits-navigator)
Designed and red-teamed an AI assistant to help SNAP recipients identify eligible foods, affordable substitutions, and simple recipes. Includes the alignment-testing process: a failure mode I found during testing (the bot drifting into general advice outside its intended scope) and how I revised the system prompt to fix it.
`Prompt Engineering` `AI Alignment` `Policy`
